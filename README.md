 I. Problem Statement

In university campuses, peak hours (lunch and dinner breaks) lead to massive overcrowding and long waiting queues at campus canteens and cafeterias. Students waste significant study and rest time standing in line just to place orders and wait for meal preparation. Furthermore, canteen staff get overwhelmed during rush hours, leading to order mix-ups and delayed service, while managers struggle to accurately forecast daily food demand.


 II. Solution Overview

Smart Student Canteen Pre-Ordering System is a full-stack web application designed for university students to browse digital menus, pre-order meals in advance, and receive unique digital tokens, allowing them to pick up their food instantly without standing in long queues.

### Key Features

* 👤 **User Accounts:** Secure student registration and login system with role-based access for students and canteen staff.
* 🍔 **Interactive Menu:** Browse available food items, daily specials, and pricing dynamically.
* 🛒 **Pre-Ordering & Cart Management:** Select meal items, adjust quantities, and submit orders in advance from anywhere on campus.
* 🔢 **Digital Token Generation:** Automatically generates a unique order token or QR code upon successful pre-order placement.
* 📊 **Real-Time Status Tracker:** Live tracking of order status (Pending, Preparing, Ready for Pick-Up, Completed).
* 👨‍🍳 **Admin Dashboard:** Enables canteen staff to view incoming orders chronologically, update statuses, and manage menu availability.
